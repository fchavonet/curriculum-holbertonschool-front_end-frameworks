The purpose of this assessment is to evaluate your understanding of **Svelte** and your ability to **compare it with React**.

The questions are **based on the concepts explored throughout the project**, the migration process, and the framework analysis performed in the previous task.

---

## Requirements:

- The assessment must be completed **individually**.
- Students must be able to **justify and explain their answers**.
- The assessment **focuses on understanding** rather than memorization.
- The assessment **contributes to the final evaluation** of the project.
