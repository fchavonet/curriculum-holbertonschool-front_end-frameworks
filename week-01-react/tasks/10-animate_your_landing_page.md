For students who would like to **go further**, you may enhance your landing page by adding **scroll-based animations**.

The goal of this task is to discover how **third-party libraries** can be integrated into a React project to **improve the user experience**.

---

- We recommend using the [AOS (Animate On Scroll)](https://github.com/michalsnik/aos) library, which provides a simple and beginner-friendly introduction to scroll-based animations.
- A [demonstration page]() has been provided to help you explore the available animations and understand how they can be used throughout the interface.
- You are free to choose:
    - Which elements to animate.
    - Which animations to use.
    - Animation duration and delay.
    - The overall animation strategy of your application.
- Be careful not to **overuse animations**.
- The objective is to improve the user experience while **maintaining readability and visual consistency**.
